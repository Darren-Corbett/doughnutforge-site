## First comment (posted by the maker)
Hello Product Hunt.

Doughnut Forge started from a simple wish: to send a private file to one person without trusting a service in the middle. It encrypts files and text on your own device. Each person has an identity in the app and a contact card to share. You encrypt a file for the people you choose, then send it however you like: email, a chat, a USB stick or cloud storage. Only they can open it.

A few things it does not do, on purpose:
- No account and no servers. Your password and keys never leave your device.
- No tracking. The app makes no network connections of its own.
- No subscription. It is a one-time purchase on each platform.

It uses post-quantum encryption, and the technical details page lists the exact methods: https://doughnut-forge.app/security/technical-details/

It has not had an independent security audit yet. One is planned, and it is on the public roadmap.

It is on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Windows today, in 30 languages. Android is coming.

Questions and criticism are welcome in the comments.
