# Site critique, 31 October 2026

Method: dual-agent Impeccable critique. A design review and the automatic detector ran separately and did not see each other's results; the top findings were then checked by hand. Findings only: no site file was changed.

- **Pages:** home, download, security, Technical details, support/FAQ, roadmap, press kit, blog index, one post (`/blog/can-whatsapp-read-my-messages/`), the iPhone `/go/` page (`/go/ef9771/en/`) and the 404 page, in English. Home and Technical details also in German at 320px.
- **Widths and themes:** 360px (WebKit, iPhone-like) and 1280px (Chromium), light and dark. Built from `main` at `02ab975` (build ID `08ae74de9e2203d4`), served locally.
- **Judged against:** `PRODUCT.md`, `DESIGN.md` and the copy rules in `CLAUDE.md`. Goals: every page as considered as the home page; an iPhone visitor gets to the right store easily.
- **Screenshots:** in `docs/design/critique-2026-10-31/`, named below.
- **Out of bounds** marks a fix that needs a new colour, font or claim. **High risk** marks a file on the CLAUDE.md list, which needs the owner's yes before any change.

**Overall:** the home page is clearly made for this product: the encryption specimen and the feature rows in the app's own tab names and colours. Most other pages are a night band, one white rounded sheet and a footer. At 1280px that sheet fills only the left 55 to 60% of the page. No page scrolls sideways at any width, German included. Heuristic score 24 of 36 (67%, "acceptable"); flexibility was not scored, because a static marketing site has no expert shortcuts to judge.

## Top 10 problems

1. **Home: the store badges have no platform names.** "Get Doughnut Forge" jumps to three stacked badges, and "App Store" and "Mac App Store" look almost the same. An iPhone owner can easily tap the Mac badge, which is the site's main job failing. Download and Pricing already label each badge with its device.
   Screenshot: `home-cta-landing.webp`.
   Fix: use the labelled platform cards from Pricing on the home page, or put "iPhone and iPad", "Mac" and "Windows" above each badge. **High risk** (`StoreLinks.astro`).

2. **Support: GitHub comes before the answers.** The heading says "Most questions are answered below". What follows is three equal blue buttons (Report a bug, Ask a question, Suggest a feature), a fourth button and a security link, and the FAQ starts about 1.5 screens down. This sends people to public GitHub issues, which need an account, before they see the answers.
   Screenshot: `support-360-light-00.webp`.
   Fix: put the FAQ first, then the GitHub section with one blue button ("Ask a question") and the others as outline buttons. **High risk** (`SupportPage.astro`).

3. **Inner pages look like a generic template.** Security, Roadmap, Technical details, Blog and the post are one left-aligned sheet with 40 to 45% of the desktop width empty. None of the home page's own patterns appear.
   Screenshots: `security-1280-dark-fold.webp`, `blog-1280-light-fold.webp`, `technical-1280-light-fold.webp`.
   Fix: give each page one pattern from DESIGN.md. Security's facts and Roadmap's Shipped, Next and Later groups as tone-ruled rows like the home features. The blog list in two columns from 1024px. **High risk** for Security (`SecurityPage.astro`).

4. **Download: the tagline makes no sense on this page.** It says "Also available for Mac and Windows." That line is reused from the home page, where it follows "Now on iPhone and iPad.", so here "also" has nothing before it.
   Screenshot: `download-360-light-fold.webp`.
   Fix: use a line the site already has, such as "Offline file encryption for Mac, iPhone, iPad and Windows." (`home.summary`).

5. **Security: the researcher's button outshouts the reassurance.** Six equal blocks sit in one sheet. The only loud button on the page is "Report a vulnerability", so an everyday visitor's eye goes to the action meant for security researchers.
   Screenshot: `security-360-light-01.webp`.
   Fix: show the four facts as rows, and put the reporting section after them with an outline button. **High risk** (`SecurityPage.astro`).

6. **Download and Pricing: the iPhone and iPad card has no requirement line.** Mac and Windows say which system version they need; iPhone and iPad says nothing. That leaves a gap in the card and leaves the main audience's question, "will it run on my phone?", unanswered.
   Screenshot: `download-360-light-fold.webp`.
   Fix: add the minimum iOS and iPadOS version, taken from the app repo's build settings as the Mac and Windows lines are. **Out of bounds** until that fact is sourced from the app repo: it is a new claim.

7. **Blog index: the titles do not look like links.** It is a text-only list with the same blue rule on every item: no colour, underline or arrow on the titles. At 1280px it fills 60% of the width and looks unfinished next to the home page.
   Screenshot: `blog-1280-light-fold.webp`.
   Fix: make each title look tappable (an ink-blue underline on hover and focus, or an arrow), and use two columns from 1024px.

8. **Press kit: a very long page on phones, and a ragged row on desktop.**
   - On phones, nine screenshots each take a full-width row, so the page is about 17,900px tall.
   - At 1280px, "iPhone: Identity" sits alone at the start of a row beside three iPad shots.

   Screenshots: `press-360-light-02.webp`, `press-1280-light.webp`.
   Fix: a two-column screenshot grid on phones, and separate iPhone and iPad groups, each under a small heading.

9. **Technical details: three small departures from DESIGN.md.**
   - The four "at a glance" cards use pastel tint fills, which DESIGN.md does not have.
   - Green marks "No network connections of its own", although DESIGN.md gives green only to "on sale" or "yours".
   - The paragraph after the "Copying an encrypted message as text" list has no space above it.
   - On phones the page is about 19,300px tall, with no way back to the contents list.

   Screenshots: `technical-360-light-03.webp`, `technical-1280-light-fold.webp`.
   Fix: plain surface cards with a 2px tone edge, blue or violet instead of green, the normal paragraph gap after lists, and a "Back to contents" link after each section.

10. **Blog post: narrow lines on phones and no way back.**
    - At 360px the sheet's edge and padding inside the page gutter cut lines to about 34 characters.
    - There is no link back to the Blog.
    - No author is shown. That is deliberate in `Post.astro`, but CLAUDE.md asks for author and date on every post.

    Screenshot: `post-360-light-00.webp`.
    Fix: drop the sheet's edge and padding below 640px, and add a small "Blog" link above the title. The author is a decision for the owner.

## Page notes

Only pages with something worth fixing beyond the top 10.

- **Home:**
  - The app screenshots show "Verified Identity" on the Identity screen (`src/assets/app/iphone-identity.webp`). That is the app's own label for the user's identity, not a claim about a sender, but it sits close to the rule "never say a file's sender is verified". **Owner decision.**
  - "Coming soon: Android. Availability depends on your country or region." is in body black, where other supporting lines use secondary slate (`home-cta-landing.webp`).
  - Only the home page carries Safari's App Store banner tag. Adding it to Download, Pricing and posts would give iPhone visitors a one-tap route. **High risk** (`Base.astro` and SEO; ask first).
- **Download:** in dark mode the official Microsoft Store badge, which is dark grey, nearly disappears into its dark card. Leave the badge artwork as it is, but more space around it would help.
- **Support:**
  - The "Privacy questions" block uses "we" and "our", repeats the GitHub instruction, and links a third-party contact form (Tally).
  - It is the privacy policy's wording, kept word for word from forge-privacy, so it is not a design fix. **High risk (legal): owner decision.**
  - The detector also flags the FAQ list's last item as sitting right against the bottom rule.
- **Roadmap:**
  - Every "Next" and "Later" item has the same violet "Planned" pill, which repeats what the group heading already says. Fix: remove the pills, or keep them only where the status differs.
  - In dark mode the group rules are near-white, heavier than the Night Line edges used everywhere else. Fix: use Night Line.

  Screenshot: `roadmap-360-light-00.webp`.
- **/go/ (iPhone):**
  - The come-back state is clear and calm: platform name, official badge, two reassurance lines and a way back (`go-360-light-nojs.webp`).
  - The violet dot before "Works without the internet." means nothing; DESIGN.md gives tab tones to features only. Fix: use blue dots, or none.
  - The "opening" state was not captured.
- **404:**
  - It works, with four ways on (`404-360-light-fold.webp`).
  - The headline "This page is encrypted. Nobody can find it." could make a first-time visitor think the site is broken. Consider a plain first line, using strings the site already has.
- **German at 320px:**
  - The header and the Technical details cards fit.
  - On the home page, "Nutzt" sits alone on a line before "Post-Quanten-Verschlüsselung," (`de-home-320-light-02.webp`). No word is broken, so it is a layout matter: let that block run a little wider at 320px.
- **Features, Pricing, Privacy, Legal:** not in scope this time.

## What works

- **The home encryption specimen.** The note turns into ciphertext, and a visitor understands the product in about three seconds. It has an honest caption, and it holds up in German at 320px.
- **The feature rows,** in the app's own tab names and colours, in both themes.
- **Honest proof built into the design:**
  - "Not audited yet" with a roadmap link.
  - Android shown as "Coming soon" with no badge or link.
  - The bold pink warning never to attach secrets.
  - The `/go/` way back.
- **The iPhone path through Download is good:**
  - Two taps from any page.
  - The iPhone card comes first.
  - The badge is in the first screen.
  - The blog posts' "Try Doughnut Forge" card goes there.
  - Download and Pricing agree: same cards, same order, same store link.

## What the detector found

183 findings across four scans (source, built pages, and pages in the browser at 1280 and 360px), 77 of them advisory. Most are not visual faults:

- **False positives:**
  - Every finding on the `/go/` page comes from Apple's App Store page. The detector followed the page's forward to apps.apple.com, so it scanned Apple's page, not the site.
  - The blue glow findings are the scan line in the home encryption animation and the brand blue.
  - The "marquee" finding is the `/go/` progress bar.
  - The "AI colour palette" finding is the app's own violet ink.
  - The repeated "(opens in a new tab)" is screen-reader text on the store links.
- **Gaps in DESIGN.md, not faults:** values the pages use but `DESIGN.md` does not record:
  - 14px and 16px text sizes;
  - the slates `#1e293b` and `#64748b`;
  - the `SFMono-Regular` code font;
  - the black frames around device screenshots.

  Worth adding the next time `DESIGN.md` is refreshed.
- **Agrees with the design review:**
  - Bordered tables inside the bordered Technical details sheets ("nested cards").
  - The FAQ's cramped bottom edge.
  - The blog post's three-step icon diagram, which matches a common template pattern. That is a design question, not a fault.
- **Single words left alone on the last line of a paragraph:** 18 at 1280px and 29 at 360px. These are advisory and change with every width and language. The site's no-word-breaking rule means some are expected.

## Limits of this run

- In the full-page phone screenshots, images that load as the visitor scrolls show as black boxes. This was checked by scrolling: they load. It is not a site fault.
- The `/go/` page at 1280px could not be captured: the forward to the store was blocked, so the shot shows the browser's error page.
- The detector's URL scan reached apps.apple.com through the `/go/` forward; its CLI cannot block outside requests.
- Playwright's WebKit and Chromium were installed locally for this run (`npx playwright install`). Nothing was added to the repo.
